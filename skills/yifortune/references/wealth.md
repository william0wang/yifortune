# YiFortune Wealth Reading

Wealth-focused analysis building on the BaZi chart. Call `get_bazi`, then apply the wealth lens. For chart structure, consult **bazi-chart**; for terminology, consult **glossary**.

## Data Source

Call `get_bazi`. Read: 正财 vs 偏财 presence and strength, day master strength (身旺/身弱), 用神 element, 财星 relationships (is wealth clashing? supported? drained?).

## Analysis Framework

### Earning Style — 正财 vs 偏财

- **正财 dominant**: stable salary, steady accumulation, salary growth. Wealth comes from diligence and consistency.
- **偏财 dominant**: investment, side business, windfall, trading. Wealth comes from opportunity and agility — but volatile.
- **Both balanced**: dual income streams — salary + investments.

### Risk Tolerance — Day Master vs. Wealth

- **身旺能担财** (strong DM + wealth present): can handle large sums, high risk tolerance, wealth-storing capacity. Favors bold moves.
- **身弱财多身弱** (weak DM + excess wealth): "wealth exceeds capacity" — money comes but slips away (overspending, bad investments, being used by others). Must strengthen self first (印星/比劫) before chasing more wealth.

### Investment Channels — Five-Element Mapping

Match the 用神 element to investment types. See **glossary** mapping-tables.md for the element → industry table (same mapping applies to investment sectors). For example:

- 用神 木 → education/agriculture/cultural sector
- 用神 火 → tech/energy/AI
- 用神 土 → real estate/infrastructure
- 用神 金 → finance/precious metals
- 用神 水 → logistics/trade/liquidity-focused

### Spending & Saving

- **食伤旺**: spends freely, emotionally — needs budgeting discipline
- **印星旺**: frugal, rational saver — may be too conservative
- **比劫旺**: spends on social connections/people — watch "人情破财"
- **财星受克**: do not lend money easily; lending leads to loss

### Financial Timing — Luck Pillars

- 财星 or 用神 大运 → wealth accumulation decade
- 比劫 大运 → wealth may be competed away (partnership disputes, overspending)
- 印星 大运 → focus on building skills/assets, wealth follows knowledge

## Output

Two layers (see SKILL.md — the plain layer is mandatory and always last):

**Inner layer (命理要点 — for traceability, compact, terms OK):**

1. **财运总论** — earning style (正财/偏财) + wealth-bearing capacity
2. **求财方式** — salary vs. investment vs. business, with reasoning
3. **投资方向** — suited channels based on 用神 element (≥3, with reasoning)
4. **理财建议** — saving/spending tendencies + specific guardrails
5. **财运时机** — current + upcoming wealth 大运 windows

**Outer layer (大白话总结 — for the user, always last, no raw jargon):**

6. **你靠什么方式赚钱最顺** — one plain sentence on their money-making style (e.g. "你是稳稳拿工资、慢慢攒的人，不太适合追风口搏一把")
7. **钱容易从哪里漏掉** — the single biggest wealth-drain tendency, in everyday terms ("你花钱容易跟着心情走，心情不好的时候特别容易乱买")
8. **接下来在钱上最该做的一两件事** — concrete financial move(s) mapped to their timing window. No "食伤生财" in this layer.

## If the user is weighing a specific money move

When the question narrows from "我怎么赚钱" (pattern) to "这一笔/这一项目该不该投" (a single decision) — 某个项目投不投、这次合伙做不做、现在该不该买房 — BaZi shows the wealth backdrop but **a Yijing cast answers that one transaction more directly**. Offer it:

> "你整体是稳扎稳打能攒钱的命。但你问的这个具体项目能不能做，我帮你起一卦看会更清楚——随便说一句话或几个词给我都行，想到什么说什么。"

See **yijing-cast** for the casting + reading flow.

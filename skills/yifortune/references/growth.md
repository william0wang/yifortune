# YiFortune Personal Growth Reading

Growth-focused analysis from the BaZi chart. For chart structure, consult **bazi-chart**; for terminology, **glossary**.

## Data Source

Call `get_bazi`. Read: dominant 十神 (talent clusters), 日主 strength (身旺/身弱), 用神/喜神, 大运 sequence (growth phases). If the user has a specific interest, hobby, or bottleneck they're working on, ask and incorporate it.

## Analysis Framework

### Talent Development — Ten Gods

| 十神              | Talent to develop                    | Growth direction                                      |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| 食伤 (expression) | Creativity, art, communication       | Build creative output habits; find expressive outlets |
| 印星 (resource)   | Learning, knowledge, depth           | Systematic study; become a domain expert              |
| 比劫 (peer)       | Networking, leadership, action       | Expand social capital; take initiative                |
| 官杀 (authority)  | Discipline, management               | Build structure; take on responsibility               |
| 财星 (wealth)     | Practical execution, resourcefulness | Turn ideas into tangible results                      |

### Growth Strategy — Day Master Strength

- **身旺**: growth comes through challenge and breaking through comfort zones. Stagnation = the enemy.
- **身弱**: growth comes through accumulation and steady building. Overextension = the enemy.

### Learning Style

- **印星旺**: systematic learner — deep reading, structured courses, long-form study. Best absorption through focused solitude.
- **食伤旺**: experiential learner — learns by doing, creating, teaching. Best absorption through output and experimentation.
- **比劫旺**: social learner — group study, discussion, peer teaching. Best absorption through interaction.

### Social Patterns

- **比劫旺**: builds wide networks easily, but watch for superficial connections (酒肉朋友). Quality over quantity as a growth focus.
- **印星旺**: prefers deep 1:1 relationships over breadth. Growth edge = expanding social radius.
- **食伤旺**: attracts others through charisma and expression. Growth edge = listening and receiving.

### Vision & Milestones

Combine the day master's nature + 用神 direction to suggest growth milestones:

- **Short-term (3-6 months)**: what to focus on now, aligned with current 大运
- **Mid-term (1-3 years)**: skill/capability to build
- **Long-term (5-10 years)**: life direction and legacy

### Bottleneck Breaking

Identify the limiting belief/pattern from the excessively strong element's negative manifestation, then prescribe the balancing approach (use the opposing element's positive quality). E.g. excessive 食伤 (scattered, over-expressive) → needs 官杀 (discipline, focus) to channel it.

## Output

Two layers (see SKILL.md — the plain layer is mandatory and always last):

**Inner layer (命理要点 — for traceability, compact, terms OK):**

1. **核心天赋** — talent clusters to leverage (from dominant 十神)
2. **成长策略** — 身旺/身弱 approach + current-phase guidance
3. **学习风格** — best knowledge-absorption method
4. **社交模式** — strengths + growth edge
5. **阶段里程碑** — short/mid/long-term focus
6. **瓶颈突破** — limiting pattern + balancing approach

**Outer layer (大白话总结 — for the user, always last, no raw jargon):**

7. **你最该使劲发展的那一点** — single biggest strength to double down on, in plain words ("你身上最值钱的是把复杂的事讲清楚的能力，往这个方向投资回报最高")
8. **你最容易卡住的模式** — the recurring bottleneck, translated to a real-life pattern ("你经常想法很多但落地的时候卡住，缺的是逼自己收尾的那股劲")
9. **接下来半年到一年，重点放在哪** — one concrete growth focus mapped to their current phase. No "身旺喜官杀" in this layer.

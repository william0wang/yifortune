# YiFortune Feng Shui & Favorable Elements

Environmental and lifestyle enhancement from the BaZi five-element profile. For chart structure, consult **bazi-chart**; for the full element → direction/color/material tables, consult **glossary** mapping-tables.md.

## Data Source

Call `get_bazi`. Read: 用神/喜神 (favorable elements), 五行 distribution (what's weak and needs environmental support). The favorable element drives all recommendations.

## Analysis Framework

### Favorable Directions

From the 用神 element, derive the person's favorable directions (see mapping-tables.md):

| Favorable element | Best direction               | Secondary                                |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| 木 Wood           | East, Southeast              | North (water generates wood)             |
| 火 Fire           | South                        | East (wood generates fire)               |
| 土 Earth          | Center, Southwest, Northeast | South (fire generates earth)             |
| 金 Metal          | West, Northwest              | Center/Southwest (earth generates metal) |
| 水 Water          | North                        | West/Northwest (metal generates water)   |

### Lucky Colors

The 用神 element's color palette (see glossary). Recommend:

- **Primary**: the favorable element's color for clothing, decor, accessories
- **Secondary**: the generating element's color (the "parent" that feeds the favorable one)

### Lucky Numbers

The 用神 element's numbers (see mapping-tables.md). Useful for phone numbers, license plates, floor selection, etc.

### Environmental Enhancement

Based on the favorable element, what to add to living/working spaces:

| Favorable element | Add to space                                                       |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 木 Wood           | Plants, wooden furniture, bamboo, green tones, botanical art       |
| 火 Fire           | Bright lighting, candles, red/purple decor, warm-toned art         |
| 土 Earth          | Ceramics, crystals, stone, yellow/beige tones, earth-toned pottery |
| 金 Metal          | Metallic objects, clocks, white/silver/gold tones, metal frames    |
| 水 Water          | Aquarium, fountain, glass, mirrors, blue/black tones, fluid shapes |

### Workspace / Desk Orientation

- **Face your favorable direction** while working (desk facing that way)
- **Avoid sitting with your back to the clash direction** (opposite of favorable)
- **Office location**: if choosing between offices, the one in your favorable sector of the building is better

### Home Arrangement

- **Bedroom**: head of bed pointing toward favorable direction
- **Front door**: if possible, facing a direction that's not your clash direction
- **Wealth corner (招财位)**: typically the diagonal corner from the entrance — enhance with the favorable element's objects
- **Romance corner (桃花位)**: based on the person's 生肖 — enhance for attracting relationships

### Travel & Relocation

- Traveling/relocating toward a favorable direction = positive energy
- Avoid relocating toward the clash direction for major moves

## Output

Two layers (see SKILL.md — the plain layer is mandatory and always last):

**Inner layer (命理要点 — for traceability, compact, terms OK):**

1. **用神方位** — favorable + clash directions with reasoning
2. **幸运元素** — colors, numbers, materials (traceable to 用神)
3. **环境建议** — what to add to living/working spaces (≥5 specifics)
4. **办公/座位** — desk orientation + office selection guidance
5. **居家布局** — bedroom, entrance, wealth/romance corner advice
6. **出行/搬迁** — direction guidance for travel/relocation

**Outer layer (大白话总结 — for the user, always last, no raw jargon):**

7. **你最有利的方位和颜色** — name the single best direction + color, in everyday words ("你最好的方位是东边，颜色是绿色系，平时多用会有帮助")
8. **办公桌/床怎么摆最顺** — one concrete placement tip, in plain spatial terms ("办公桌尽量让自己面朝东坐；床头朝东，睡眠会更踏实")
9. **家里/工位上可以加点什么** — 2-3 concrete objects to add, by everyday name ("桌上放一盆绿植，家里东边那个角落可以放点木质的摆件"). No "用神补木/桃花位催旺" in this layer unless glossed.
